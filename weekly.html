The Week in Macro

  • An oil shock hit, and bonds rallied anyway. WTI rose 13.1% in a week, from $95.39 to $107.89. Normally that would push yields up on inflation fears. Instead the 10-year Treasury yield fell 14bp to 4.96% and the 2-year fell 12bp to 4.65%. Markets are treating expensive oil as a hit to growth more than an inflation threat.
  • Prediction markets backed the bond rally. The odds of the 10-year hitting 5.5% before 2027 fell from 26.7% to 12.25%. The odds of 5.7% fell from 12.95% to 5.9%. Bettors are pulling back from the "yields keep climbing" scenario.

  • Global inflation expectations eased. Markets now lean towards lower 2026 inflation in both the UK and China. That supports the idea that rate pressure is fading worldwide, even with oil higher.
  • Appetite for risk faded slightly. The odds of the S&P 500 reaching 8,200 in December fell from 30.5% to 21%. Financial conditions barely moved (Chicago Fed index −0.502 → −0.496) and remain loose. The broad dollar slipped 0.16%.
