The Week in Macro

*Markets marked down growth and risk this week, but not enough to signal recession.*

  • Growth expectations cooled. The favored range for 2026 US growth is now 2.0–2.5%, as bets on anything faster faded. October data are mixed: payrolls rose 165,000, unemployment dipped to 4.1% and housing starts jumped 9.9%, but industrial production fell 0.4%.
