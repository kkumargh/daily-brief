  • On AI’s economic impact: Some view AI as a transformative growth engine—evidenced by Cadence’s record $8.1B backlog ([11]) and Meta’s Muse scaling to 3.6B users ([7])—while others argue it enables cost-cutting and displacement, particularly in creative industries where over 200k jobs have been lost ([68]), or warn that current valuations reflect narrative-driven bubbles rather than fundamentals ([34], [71]).

Non-Consensus / Contrarian Flag

With higher rates here to stay, the author predicts a violent repricing across private markets, SPACs, and unprofitable tech—directly challenging mainstream optimism around AI and innovation-led growth. This stands in stark contrast to bullish agent narratives ([43]) and deep-value plays banking on continued liquidity.
