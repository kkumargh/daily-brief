Alpha in Academia · Jul 18

Flexible Forwards in Time-Dependent Models:

Andersen, Itkin, and Kazbek show that the optimal exercise trigger for flexible forwards does not rise linearly with variance, as previously assumed, but bends over time. This new pricing method, which is significantly faster and more accurate, can value the timing option in flexible forwards at 271 pips, far above typical quotes of 50-100 pips, highlighting a potential market inefficiency.

Corporate Bond Dealers Aren't Just Plumbing:

Geilen finds that corporate bond dealers' "axes" (buy/sell signals) predict future bond performance, with bonds dealers want to buy outperforming those they want to sell by 25 basis points over a month. This suggests dealers' signals carry valuable market information, particularly in high-yield markets, where fewer analysts are involved.

Steadier Factors: Anchoring Rolling PCA With Prior Exposures:

Nakagawa, Kato, and Imamura demonstrate that nudging rolling factor models toward economically sensible starting points makes them more stable, reducing factor drift by 25% and lowering portfolio turnover without sacrificing accuracy. This approach can lower trading costs and provide more reliable factor attributions.
