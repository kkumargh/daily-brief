Engrams Embedding Entendre: Codesign for Efficient DRAM/SSD Offloading

SemiAnalysis · Sep 18

Engram’s architecture reduces HBM demand by offloading learned multi-token embeddings to DRAM or SSD, enabling more efficient memory use without sacrificing model quality—this shifts focus from HBM capacity to bandwidth, favoring 4-hi HBM stacks for cost-effective inference. Despite theoretical benefits of SSD offloading, current implementations underperform DRAM due to CPU round-trips and coordination overhead, with B200-based systems showing up to 2.3x lower tokens-per-dollar versus DRAM; NVIDIA’s CUDA moat and day-one vLLM support across H100/H200/B200/B300 further solidify its inference dominance over AMD’s MI355X, which lags up to 42x in performance per dollar. Actionable view: Favor NVIDIA GPU SKUs (especially B200/B300) for Engram workloads, prioritize HBM bandwidth over capacity, and avoid SSD offloading until optimized kernels close the performance gap.
