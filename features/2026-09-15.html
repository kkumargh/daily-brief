A Brain Too Big to Carry — On-Device vs Datacenter Inference

SemiAnalysis · Sep 14

The core debate in robotics is whether intelligence should reside on-device or in the datacenter, with real-time control and cost constraints favoring smaller onboard models (~3–14B parameters), while scaling demands and silicon efficiency increasingly favor offloading compute to shared datacenter GPUs. Offloading the planning layer (≤20 Hz) to datacenter GPUs like NVIDIA’s B300 becomes economically and materially superior at scale—crossing over at ~5–7 robots per GPU in DRAM and wafer efficiency—despite network jitter challenges. Our view: hybrid architectures will dominate, but for generalist robots, datacenter-offloaded inference wins on TCO and scalability, benefiting NVIDIA (NVDA) as both edge and datacenter demand grow, though supply chain bottlenecks in leading-edge nodes and DRAM will constrain near-term robot volume ramps.
