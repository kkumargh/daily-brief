Conversely, [27] warns of weakening foreign appetite (e.g., Norway’s potential $80B cut) and rising issuance pressure, especially from Malaysia and the U.S.—implying upward yield pressure that contradicts the stablecoin-driven demand thesis.

Non-Consensus / Contrarian Flag

The most contrarian call is [7]’s emphasis on advanced packaging as the true bottleneck in AI, not wafer fabrication. While most AI investing focuses on GPU makers or foundry capacity, this view identifies substrate, interposer, and thermal solution suppliers—hidden enablers of 2.5D/3D integration—as the critical, underappreciated layer with high switching costs and durable margins. This shifts investment focus downstream from ASML/NVIDIA to less-followed industrial and materials players in the packaging stack.
