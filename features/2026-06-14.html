Alpha in Academia · Jun 13

The Boltzmann Equation in Finance: Researchers have derived a closed-form expression for the probability distribution of a European call option's payoff using the Boltzmann equation, decomposing it into a spike at zero and a log-normal distribution for positive payoffs. This provides a more comprehensive view for risk management, consistent with Black-Scholes while offering deeper insights into tail risk. Ticker: GOOGL (Alphabet Inc.)

Cross-market risk spillovers in Belt and Road Initiative economies: Financial markets in BRI economies show tight stock market integration and segmented bond markets, with Singapore and Eastern European economies exporting risk, while China absorbs it. This pattern is consistent across stock, bond, and currency markets, with China acting as a shock absorber despite its economic size. Actionable Insight: China's financial markets are more resilient to external shocks, making them a stable component in BRI-focused portfolios.

Strategies for Dynamic Bond Ladder Portfolios: A dynamic bond ladder strategy, treating each rung as an optimal stopping decision, can reduce drawdown and tail risk compared to passive ladders, especially during yield curve transitions.
