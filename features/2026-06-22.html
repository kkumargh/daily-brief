Subscriber Letter #14: The Markowitz Model

The Onveston Letter · Jun 21

Harry Markowitz's 1952 paper introduced Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT), emphasizing that investments should be evaluated based on their impact on the entire portfolio rather than individually. By combining assets with low correlation, investors can reduce overall portfolio risk without sacrificing expected returns, a concept Markowitz famously termed the "free lunch" of diversification. For example, a 60% stock (8% return, 18% volatility) and 40% bond (4% return, 7% volatility) portfolio can achieve a 6.4% expected return with volatility below 13.6% if the assets are not perfectly correlated.
