This can lead to counterintuitive outcomes: setting reserve prices where marginal revenue hits zero ($5 for a bidder valuing 0–10, $15 for 10–30), and even selling to lower-value bidders when their marginal revenue exceeds that of higher bidders (e.g., A at $8 wins over B at $17 after adjusting bids by $10). Actionable takeaway: in constrained supply settings, sellers maximize revenue by using reserve prices and bid adjustments that equalize marginal revenues across bidders—principles applicable to spectrum auctions, IPOs, or any scarce asset sale.
