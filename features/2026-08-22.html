On Schmuck Insurance

The Hermit · Aug 19

Core Thesis/Argument: "Schmuck Insurance" is a mechanism in M&A deals that protects sellers from missing out on significant future gains if the company they sold performs exceptionally well post-sale. This concept can be applied in portfolio management by allocating a small percentage of capital to high-risk, high-reward investments to hedge against regret and capture potential outsized returns.

Actionable Market View: Consider allocating 0.1% to 0.5% of your portfolio to speculative bets like extreme out-of-the-money options, low-probability setups, or tail-risk scenarios (e.g., 2-year dated Nasdaq puts when the VIX is below 15) to protect against regret and potentially benefit from extraordinary payouts.
