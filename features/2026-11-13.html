The great economic theorist, Richard Posner (1939-2026)

Economic Forces · Nov 12

Richard Posner pioneered the economic analysis of law by arguing that common law evolves toward economic efficiency, offering a unified framework to explain diverse legal outcomes—reducing the need for case-specific models. His approach implies that markets and regulations should be analyzed through consistent economic principles, suggesting regulatory barriers (like postal monopolies) often exist to protect cross-subsidization schemes, not just competition. No direct ticker, but the insight favors skepticism toward fragmented regulatory outcomes and supports efficiency-driven legal predictability in market-impacting rulings.
