  • AI spending trajectory: [10] argues LLM spend is cooling as enterprises shift to ROI-driven use cases, while [1] and [50] emphasize accelerating agentic AI adoption and infrastructure buildout, suggesting continued capex growth. This reflects a core tension between demand-side softness and supply-side momentum.

In contrast, [42] reframes the national debt as a non-issue for a sovereign currency issuer, implying Treasuries may be unduly discounted—directly opposing the fiscal doom narrative.

This MMT-adjacent view, which implies long-duration Treasuries (e.g., TLT) are structurally undervalued, stands in stark contrast to the fiscal alarmism in [13] and [45].
