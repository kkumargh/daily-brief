Is AI Replacing Programmers or Boosting Them?

Justified Posteriors · Jul 27

The paper "Writing Code vs. Shipping Code" by Demirer, Musolff, and Yang finds that while AI coding tools can increase lines of code written by over 100%, the economic value (measured by shipped releases) only increases by about 30%. The authors estimate an elasticity of substitution of 0.25 between AI and human effort, suggesting strong complementarity, which could imply that AI boosts human wages and job creation rather than replacing them. This has significant long-term implications for the tech sector and labor markets. Ticker: MSFT (Microsoft, a key data provider in the study).
