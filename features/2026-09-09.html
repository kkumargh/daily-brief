Dominant Themes

  • AI infrastructure bottlenecks are shifting investment focus to underutilized "toll roads"—companies with latent capacity in constrained segments like optical interconnects (1.6T transceivers), power semiconductors, and HBM/wafer supply. Multiple sources highlight that while demand visibility is multi-year, only firms with headroom—not fully booked players—are attractively positioned ([9], [4]).

Meta’s Muse agent launch exemplifies this shift toward persistent, proactive consumer agents ([3], [54]).

These events are no longer isolated but seen as interconnected flashpoints affecting equities in energy, defense, and semiconductors ([52], [37], [63]).
  • Valuation frameworks are adapting to AI-era capital intensity, with emphasis on normalized growth, ROIIC, and excess FCF rather than simplistic multiples. Quality businesses with durable reinvestment capacity (e.g., high ROIC + scalable FCF) justify premiums even amid multiple compression, while mispricings arise from "optical" issues masking true earnings power ([12], [33]).
